Jack's Bagels Mossel Bay - Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Jack's Bagels Mossel Bay collects when you join our specials list or enter a promotion, why we collect it, and how you can control it. Last updated: 22 November 2026.

1. Who we are

This site and our social media pages are run by Jack Bagels Mossel Bay (Pty) Ltd (registration number 2021/469213/07), trading as Jack's Bagels at Marsh Street, Langeberg Mall and Hartenbos, Mossel Bay (35 Marsh Street, Mossel Bay). We are the "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Jack's Bagels is a franchise. This policy covers only the three Mossel Bay stores that we run, and the specials list we keep for them.

Information Officer: Riaan Roode, marketing.mb@jacksbagels.co.za, 083 459 4519.

2. What we collect

  • Your first name and email address, given through our Facebook/Instagram sign-up form or in store.
  • Your cell number, only if we ask for it in future and you choose to give it. We currently collect email only.
  • The store you prefer and which promotion or ad brought you to us.
  • The date and time you agreed to hear from us, so we can prove consent.
  • Basic page and ad statistics from Meta and our analytics tools. These do not identify you personally.

We do not collect ID numbers, card details or information about children. You must be 18 or older to join our list.

3. Why we use it

  • To send you specials, new menu items and promotions from Jack's Bagels Mossel Bay.
  • To see which promotions bring people into our stores.
  • To run competitions you entered and contact winners.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to the above.

4. Your consent and opting out

We only send marketing if you opted in. You can withdraw at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or emailing marketing.mb@jacksbagels.co.za. We stop within 5 business days. Withdrawing does not affect anything sent before.

5. Who sees your information

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram), where you completed the form.
  • HubSpot, our email and contact system. Its servers are in the European Union.
  • WaRCoM IT Solutions (Pty) Ltd, our IT service provider, who manage our systems for us.
  • Delivery or competition partners only where a promotion says so.

These providers may process your data outside South Africa. We only use providers that protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your details while you stay subscribed, and delete them within 24 months of your last interaction or on request, whichever is first. Records proving your consent are kept for as long as we hold your details.

7. How we protect it

Access is limited to named staff with passwords and two-step sign-in where available. Our systems use encryption in transit. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what we hold on you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email marketing.mb@jacksbagels.co.za. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforeg@justice.gov.za or https://inforegulator.org.za.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
